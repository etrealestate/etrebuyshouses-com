Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 28, 2026. This policy explains what this website collects, why, and what choices you have. It covers this website only.

Information we collect

Information you give us. When you fill out a form on this site, we collect what you enter: your name, phone number, email address (if you provide one), and property details such as the address, property type, and your ideal timeline.

Information collected automatically. When you visit this site, we and our advertising partner may collect browser and device information and pages visited, through cookies and similar technologies, including the Meta Pixel described below.

How we use it

We use the information to respond to your inquiry, evaluate your property, and communicate with you about a potential sale. We also use visit information to measure how the site is performing and to show ads to people who have visited the site.

Cookies and the Meta Pixel

This site uses the Meta Pixel, a tool from Meta Platforms, Inc. The Pixel places cookies in your browser (such as _fbp and _fbc) that record pages you visit on this site. Meta uses that information to measure visits and to show you ET Real Estate ads on Facebook and Instagram after you leave this site. The information collected through the Pixel is browser and device information and pages visited; it does not include your name or phone number unless you enter them in a form.

Meta's use of this information is governed by Meta's own privacy policy. You can learn more about Meta's data practices and adjust your ad preferences in your Facebook or Instagram ad settings.

Google tags. This site also uses Google tags for advertising measurement. Google may place cookies (such as _gac_*) that record visits and ad interactions, used to measure ad performance and to show you ET Real Estate ads on Google Search and partner sites (remarketing). Google's use of this information is governed by Google's privacy policy.

Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We share information with Meta through the Pixel and with Google through Google tags, in each case for the advertising and measurement purposes described above, and with service providers that help operate this website and handle inquiries, such as the service that delivers form submissions to us.

Your choices

You can opt out of interest-based advertising from Meta in your Facebook or Instagram ad settings, and out of Google's personalized advertising in your Google Ad Settings (adssettings.google.com). You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings, though some parts of the site may not work as expected without them. You can also opt out of interest-based ads from many advertisers through the Digital Advertising Alliance at optout.aboutads.info. If you would rather not use the form, you can call us at 618-720-0076.

Children

This site is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to etrealestate.homes@gmail.com or by calling 618-720-0076.
